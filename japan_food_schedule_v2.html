🌱 Vegan dining in Japan — read this first

Dashi is the hidden problem. Fish stock (dashi) is used as a base in an enormous number of dishes that look vegetarian — miso soup, udon broth, ramen broth, rice seasonings, even some sauces. Many restaurant staff won't know this is an issue unless specifically asked.

Carry a Japanese vegan dining card. Search "vegan Japan restaurant card" — Happy Cow and The Vegan Society both have free printable versions in Japanese. This single tool makes the biggest difference at any restaurant.

Each option below is tagged: 🌱 vegan ok means confirmed vegan-friendly options exist. ⚠ ask about dashi means the cuisine type is risky and the card is essential. ✗ vegan unlikely means the vegan person will probably need to eat elsewhere or get something separate.
