🌱 Updated vegan restrictions — read this

What's now OK: dashi (fish stock), bone broth, lard, and other animal-derived cooking fats and stocks. This removes the biggest barrier to eating in Japan. He can now eat most Japanese broths, tempura dipping sauces, miso soup, plain udon and soba, most izakaya vegetable dishes, and Japanese-style pickles.

Still not OK: meat, fish, seafood, poultry, eggs, dairy. Gyoza filling, sushi, ramen toppings (chashu pork, egg), most yakitori, tonkatsu, any dish where meat/fish/egg is a primary component.

Custom card: The standard Japanese vegan card will over-flag dashi and lard. He should carry a custom card saying: "I do not eat meat, fish, seafood, poultry, eggs, or dairy. Animal-based stocks and fats (dashi, lard, bone broth) are fine." Build one at HappyCow's card builder.
